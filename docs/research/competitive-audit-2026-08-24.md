# Competitive audit — Organize, Guide, and Connect implications

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-24 PT
**Purpose:** Preserve the evidence behind the WORDLESS Relay decision
**Confidence boundary:** Guide winners were not yet announced

## Executive finding

The public evidence supports the project owner's concern that a sophisticated
build can lose to a simpler entry when the simple entry makes its user, action,
and payoff obvious immediately. The Organize placements reward a familiar
problem, a spatially necessary action, a visible object-state transformation,
and a submission surface that compresses into one sentence.

WORDLESS Relay is competitive only if it remains that simple. The browser must
visibly cause the Lens result, and the exact drawing must visibly become the
session glyph. A generic air-drawing demo or a decorative companion website
would erase its advantage.

## PackSpace pattern

PackSpace's browser companion creates a categorized packing list and a short
code. The wearer imports the list and places spatial packing zones. The
important pattern is not “add a website.” It is:

> A second actor contributes task-critical information in the interface natural
> to them; the wearer performs the location-dependent spatial work.

Repository: <https://github.com/r3d5/PackSpace>

## Sensei escalation

Sensei, published from the same GitHub account as PackSpace, expands that
pattern into a multi-role guide workflow:

```text
Lens instructor records
  -> browser author enriches layout and media
  -> Lens reviewer approves
  -> trainee follows the spatial guide
```

The website is causally necessary because its authored materials and status
change what the trainee receives. Public strengths include a substantial
authoring UI, media/backend model, role separation, review state, visual polish,
and a detailed CLAD record.

Public limitations include Preview-only proof, session-local marker behavior,
permissive hackathon security posture, one squashed public commit, no obvious
automated test suite, and an unfilled demo-link field in the repository's
submission document. These limit production claims but do not erase the strong
submission story.

- Repository: <https://github.com/r3d5/sensei-spatial-guide>
- Hosted authoring surface: <https://sensei-guide-sand.vercel.app/>

No Guide placement is attributed to Sensei in this audit because official
Guide winners were scheduled for 2026-08-27.

## FIRST BOUNCE comparison

FIRST BOUNCE had serious engineering discipline and a distinctive spatial
model, but its viewer had to learn several concepts before the full value was
clear: first reflections, multiple seats, modeled coverage, panel placement,
and the status grammar. That cognitive load is a competitive risk even when the
implementation is sound.

The lesson carried into Connect is not “build less code.” It is “make the user,
action, and payoff readable before the implementation story begins.” WORDLESS
Relay therefore opens with the two participants already interacting, not with
setup or an explanation of realtime architecture.

## Connect convergence audit

Air drawing, charades, and Pictionary-like guessing are predictable LLM idea
families. Prior art prevents a credible first-ever claim. The concept remains
defensible through a narrow combination:

1. One wearer draws a body-scale spatial clue.
2. A non-wearer participates from an ordinary browser.
3. The browser's choice causes an immediate Lens-side result.
4. The exact path, not an AI-generated illustration, becomes a shared session
   medallion.

Removing item 2 makes it generic AR drawing. Removing item 3 makes the browser
a dashboard. Removing item 4 removes the memorable payoff.

## Final concept ranking after audit

| Rank | Concept | Competitive advantage | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | WORDLESS Relay | Emotional, cross-platform, immediate causality | Convergent game family and alpha realtime gate |
| 2 | Split the Table | Familiar utility and obvious multi-person value | A phone already solves most of the task |
| 3 | WORDLESS Duo | Strong shared spatial interaction | Colocated only and requires two Spectacles clients |
| 4 | Warp & Weft | Distinct collaborative visual magic | Lower utility and higher explanation cost |

The approved choice is WORDLESS Relay, conditional on the recorded feasibility
spike in the design specification.
