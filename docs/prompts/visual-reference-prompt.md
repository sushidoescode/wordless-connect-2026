# WORDLESS Relay visual-reference prompt

**Used by the project owner with:** Gemini Nano Banana and ChatGPT image
generation
**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Purpose:** Non-shipping visual development reference

```text
Create one finished 1920×1080 product-design hero image for an original
Snap Spectacles connected experience called WORDLESS RELAY.

This is a polished visual-development reference for a real Lens Studio and
web application, not an advertisement and not a sci-fi concept.

Show a clean split composition:

LEFT, approximately 65 percent:
A believable first-person augmented-reality view inside a simple warm,
modern living room. No visible hands and no headset frame. A thick,
luminous ultraviolet-violet ribbon is being painted freely through the
air, forming a simple flowing snake-like silhouette. Its active drawing
tip is a small warm-lemon comet with a subtle trail. Include only minimal
spatial UI: a compact label reading “DRAW: SNAKE”, a restrained round
timer, and one small connection indicator. The ribbon must feel truly
three-dimensional and located in the room, not drawn flat on the image.

RIGHT, approximately 35 percent:
A highly polished responsive browser game interface on a deep-plum
background. Heading: “GUESS THE CLUE”. Show the same live violet ribbon
projected clearly in the browser. Beneath it are four large accessible
answer cards: SNAKE, RIVER, ROPE, WAVE. Show SNAKE in a confident
mint-green correct state. Make the cards understandable from several feet
away and suitable for a phone or laptop.

PAYOFF:
Visually suggest that the loose violet scribble is snapping into a small,
beautiful emblem or glyph shared by both interfaces. The transformation
should be the emotional magic moment.

Art direction:
premium playful product design, warm and human, simple enough to
understand in five seconds, excellent spacing and typography, restrained
depth, subtle bloom, tactile rounded controls, strong hierarchy, no
clutter. Use ultraviolet violet, warm lemon, mint, coral, warm ivory, and
deep plum. Avoid orange-and-cyan engineering graphics, amber enterprise
branding, generic cyberpunk, holographic dashboards, neon grids,
metaverse clichés, fantasy environments, branded devices, third-party
logos, watermarks, excessive copy, illegible text, or visible hands.

The result should look achievable in Lens Studio and a modern web app.
Prioritize interaction clarity and production realism over cinematic
spectacle. Render one coherent finished product frame, not a storyboard,
mood board, or collection of variations.
```

## Interpretation rule

The generated images are aspirational composition studies. The approved
production design replaces their semantic snake-icon transformation with the
exact path normalized into a deterministic medallion and removes effects that
cannot be proven in Lens Studio Preview.
